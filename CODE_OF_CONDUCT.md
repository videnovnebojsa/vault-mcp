# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged behaviour:**

- Being respectful and constructive in feedback and discussion
- Gracefully accepting differing opinions and experiences
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments in any form
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a private [security advisory](https://github.com/videnovnebojsa/vault-mcp/security/advisories/new) or by emailing the maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour deemed harmful.
